The fixed action list, two playback modes and waveform dominate; the actual app is restricted to a smaller pane and extends below the viewport. Verdict: fail for the requested explanatory task; baseline only.
- `screen-studio-reference.png`: 1440 × 1000, external Screen Studio page, dark. Readable single-focus explanation and contextual controls illustrate framing. Cookie notice and animated content were present. Verdict: inspected reference, not our product or a claim that all of that site passes review. Its decorative marketing treatments are not adopted.
- `video-frame-4s.png`: 1280 × 900, original video frame extracted with FFmpeg at four seconds. The support inbox, real delivery retry message, search and conversation are readable. Verdict: pass; establishes that the historical video contains the correct app. The black browser capture came from incomplete media verification and missing range support in the test server, not a missing original recording.

The reference screenshot is retained as bounded design research, not republished on the website. No native OS-input or assistive-technology review.
