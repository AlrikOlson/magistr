# Graph and project-map review

Accepted for the pictured headless WebKit states. Every one of the 16 dense-graph and project-map images below was opened from this archive and inspected. The other 17 images are byte-for-byte identical to the final focused captures; their actual reviews are linked individually.

The canvas viewport now ends above its controls. At actual size, large graphs intentionally continue beyond the viewport; Fit all provides an overview, and selection opens complete content. Compact overviews preserve readable primary type and omit secondary facts. No line crosses decision text, and membership connections do not imply work dependencies.

These captures retain their original source-bound receipts. Subsequent changes affected the conversation reply outline; the 17 shared focused images match iteration 21 exactly, and the final 124-test run covers the current project-map and graph implementations. Native input and assistive-technology behavior remain untested in this run.
