# Product manual — visual review 06

Verdict: **PASS**. Reviewed 2026-10-21 22:11:09 UTC.

All 118 images were opened from this folder at original resolution. This includes every one of the 21 manual pages at 1280 × 1000 and 390 × 844, in light and dark appearance, plus open navigation, search results/empty/failure, contents, code/table detail, resize focus, 320-pixel reflow and 200% text. Each entry below records the reader's task and concrete inspection. Browser assertions and source hashes alone were not treated as visual review.

Renderer: Playwright WebKit 27.2. Capture times, viewport, appearance, full-frame status, image hashes and rendering/test source hashes are in [docs-browser-checks.json](docs-browser-checks.json); per-image review metadata is in [review.json](review.json).

The desktop active link is visible on direct entry, including the lower maintenance/contributor pages. Compact navigation expands in normal flow, and search remains outside that disclosure. Tables wrap rather than forcing horizontal page scrolling. Links, command panels, prompts, headings and related pages remain legible in both appearances. The 200% captures show proportional enlargement of paragraphs, inline links, emphasis, code and table text.

The earlier 200% capture failure was traced to WebKit's delayed inherited font-size recalculation after a full-page capture and resize. The test now waits for measured descendant font sizes to reach twice their baseline before judging or capturing enlarged text. No CSS workaround was added. Attempts 03–05 preserve their interrupted or failed status; they are not passing evidence.

Limits: headless browser rendering and DOM input only. Native operating-system input, VoiceOver/other assistive technology, and native desktop application behavior were not tested. There are no graph connections or branch-routing visuals in this website change.
