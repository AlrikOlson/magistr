## Verification and limits

The browser checks exercised all 47 actual actions, source-file reconstruction, failed and passed checks, milestone navigation, keyboard and pointer scrubbing, canvas selection, play/pause/speed, video metadata and seeking, downloads, load failure and the no-JavaScript transcript. Content and integrity checks independently reconstruct all nine delivered source files from recorded writes and edits and compare the downloaded artifacts with their manifest.

The app video is an unmodified 12-second recording of the completed inbox. The five-minute build is a timestamped recording of real tool actions and results, with idle pauses shortened during playback; it is not a continuous terminal video. The full-size video link is available because detailed app text is necessarily small in a compact embedded preview. The app's own readable desktop/compact states were inspected in [the app review](../2026-10-20-inbox-build-refined/REVIEW.md).

Headless WebKit does not establish native Safari/Chrome/Firefox, physical touch, screen-reader, or OS-level input coverage. No foreground OS-input test was run. The canvas is supplemental to the semantic range, milestone buttons and event list; this view has no dependency connector lines or 3D scene requiring geometry review.
