Desktop design, edition 10

Edition 9 rebuilt the desktop from scratch on the design contract (docs/design/desktop/DESIGN.md), the product brief (PRODUCT.md) and a critique of edition 8 that scored it 25 of 40. This edition fixes the slip-ups found in it. Every one of the 28 journeys has a screen below. Use Night and Day to switch every screen at once.

What edition 10 fixes

Slip-ups found in edition 9, by you and by a screen-by-screen audit. Each fix is made where the component is defined, so every screen gets it.

Slip-up in edition 9Fix in edition 10
The status column’s divider moved from row to row, because each row sized its own status cellA list is one grid: every row shares its columns, so the dividers line up in every list
Focus rings and selection outlines were clipped square by the list’s rounded cornersThe first and last rows take the list’s corner radius, so a ring or outline follows the curve
“Add a project” was always highlighted: a class meant for added lines in a diff matched it tooDiff classes are named for the diff only; the sidebar item has no fill
Four selected requests drew four ember outlinesChoosing several is shown by the checkbox and a raised row; the outline means the one item open in the sheet
Task marks on things that are not tasks: merge requests, code hosts, a decisionMarks are for tasks only. Requests, merges, hosts and notes have their own icons
Monospace for counts, dates and times (“8 s ago”)Monospace only for ids, paths, machine names and code; numbers are Plex Sans with tabular figures
A browser-blue link and tables at 16 pxLinks take the text colour with a quiet underline; tables use the 13 px body size
Short frames cut the sidebar off before Find and SettingsFrames are tall enough, and the sidebar scrolls when a window is short
The small window’s action bar floated mid-screenThe list fills the window and the action bar sits at the bottom
The Undo notice sat across the list and the sheetNotices belong to the column that caused them
“Responsible person on the right”, an instruction inside a screenThe responsible person is shown with the person icon in the status cell

Edition 9 already answered the critique of edition 8 (25 of 40): no coloured edge bars, one way to move around, contrast-safe text, drawn icons, Start buttons, confirmation and Undo, a first-run page and freshness in the sidebar.
